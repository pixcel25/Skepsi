This year has been the best year of my career, but I still feel unhappy despite achieving goals that were once a dream. I don't feel excited.

## But why?

I realise it's mainly due to two reasons: trying to optimise everything and constantly being doomerish.

Society has taught me that the only way of living life is by always being productive. You have to grind, avoid wasting your 20s on pleasures, and save your money.

I saved the money I earned and avoided spending it. The more I did this, the more unhappy I became. I had forgotten why I wanted money in the first place. I wanted it so I could spend it on trips with my friends.

I began spending my money. I went to trips with my friends and bought a good pair of IEMs.

The second issue is something I developed because of the internet: the idea that the world is doomed. I worry a lot about my future. All these heavy thoughts make me very doomerish, and I cannot enjoy things I used to love.

## The toll

All this led to me paying the price. Months of ignoring my mental health and not being ready to change left me in a worse mental state.

I have to fix this before it's too late. I cannot let society convince me that my life is a waste. I have to go to Spain, Sweden, and do many things left on my bucket list.

## The fix

I realised that the best time to live is now. Not tomorrow, not in the future. Yes, you have to prepare for the future, but that does not mean ruining your present. They say you should work hard in your prime to save for retirement, but what if I don't want to do that? I want to enjoy my 20s and 30s, living the life I want, rather than waiting until my 60s, when I can barely run.

Recently, I started appreciating people through gestures I wouldn't normally make, as it's supposedly not "manly" to appreciate people. You have to be this "alpha male" who hides his emotions, but the fact is, the more you hold them in, the more it hurts you. One day, it will be too late.

## Relax

I also realised that it's okay if you do nothing, yes, absolutely nothing. I know it feels like the biggest waste of time, and you would rather scroll or work, but doing nothing is more productive than faking work just to pass the time.

One thing that helped me a lot was leaving the shithole that is X, formerly Twitter. It affects you more negatively than any other social media platform. Instagram can also unintentionally make you doomerish. The algorithm is designed to retain you. If you watch one doomer video, your entire feed is suddenly full of doomer videos.

I suggest removing social media from your daily life and remembering that everything has a toll, a price to pay.
